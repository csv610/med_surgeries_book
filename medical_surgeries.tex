% Preface
\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}
\markboth{Preface}{Preface}

Modern surgical practice encompasses a vast array of operative techniques, from minimally invasive endoscopic procedures to complex open reconstructive surgeries. This book focuses exclusively on surgical procedures --- operations performed in the operating room, procedure suites, and interventional labs that involve incision, excision, reconstruction, transplantation, or structural alteration of human anatomy.

\textbf{Scope:} This book covers surgical procedures organized alphabetically by procedure name. Non-surgical diagnostic tests, imaging studies, medical therapies (chemotherapy, radiation, immunotherapy), and bedside procedures (lumbar puncture, central line placement, endoscopy without surgical intervention) are outside the scope of this volume.

\textbf{Organization:} Individual surgical procedures are presented in a standardized format covering: What Is This Surgery?, Alternative Names, Principle, History, Why This Surgery Is Performed, Primary vs Secondary Procedure, How This Surgery Is Performed, What Preparation Is Needed, Contraindications and Precautions, Risks, What Happens After the Surgery, Understanding Results, Normal Results, and Follow-up Care.
